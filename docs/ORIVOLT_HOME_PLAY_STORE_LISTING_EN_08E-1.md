### Full description

Orivolt Home is an Android launcher for organizing and personalizing your home screen.

Create and adjust home screens with shortcuts. Browse your apps in a drawer with categories, an alphabetical index, and search. Add widgets from apps you choose and view a system RAM indicator.

Personalize the interface with style options, Spanish or English language selection, and backgrounds or icons you select from your device. If you do not select a background, Orivolt Home shows an included static gradient.

Hidden Apps lets you remove selected apps from the drawer, categories, and search. You can protect access using options available on your device or a credential configured for this feature.

Optionally, you can grant notification access to show a pending-message counter. You can also enable the Accessibility Service to lock the screen with a double tap and open the notification panel with a gesture or from the counter. Before Android Settings opens, Orivolt Home explains this purpose and lets you accept or cancel. The service does not retrieve window content or process accessibility events.

Some features depend on components that you choose or enable, such as widgets from other apps, Android's picker, notification access, and accessibility. Read the privacy policy: https://mikies21-programmer.github.io/OrivoltHome-Privacy/
